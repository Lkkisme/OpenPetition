# Forms, Lockbox Rejection Prevention & RFE/NOID Playbook

A strong petition brief will still be rejected at the USCIS Lockbox if a form edition is outdated, the Asylum Program Fee checkbox is missed, or an NIW packet omits the mandatory uncertified ETA-9089 components. Always verify live primary sources on `uscis.gov` before finalizing `final/05-filing-checklist.md`.

---

## 1. Form I-140 Self-Petitioner Line-by-Line Trap Checklist

In an EB-1A or EB-2 NIW self-petition, the **Petitioner** (Part 1) and the **Beneficiary** (Part 3) are the **same individual**. Check these critical items against the current Form I-140 and instructions:

1. **Part 1 — Information About the Person or Organization Filing This Petition:**
   - Complete **Items 1.a–1.c** (Individual's Family Name, Given Name, Middle Name). Leave **Item 2** (Company or Organization Name) and **Item 3** (IRS EIN) blank.
   - **Part 1, Items 5 & 6 — The $300 Asylum Program Fee Self-Petitioner Rule (89 FR 6194):**
     - Under the USCIS Fee Rule (effective April 1, 2024 and current in 2026), individual self-petitioners qualify for the reduced **$300 Small Employer Asylum Program Fee** (instead of $600).
     - To prevent automatic Lockbox rejection, an individual self-petitioner must check:
       - **Part 1, Item 5** (*Are you a nonprofit...?*): **`No`**
       - **Part 1, Item 6** (*Do you currently employ a total of 25 or fewer full-time equivalent employees...?*): **`Yes`**
     - Leaving Items 5–6 blank or checking "No" to Item 6 while enclosing $300 triggers an immediate Lockbox rejection of the entire package.

2. **Part 2 — Petition Type:**
   - **EB-1A:** Check **`1.a`** (*An alien of extraordinary ability*).
   - **EB-2 NIW:** Check **`1.h`** (*An alien applying for a National Interest Waiver who is a member of the professions holding an advanced degree or an alien of exceptional ability*). Do **not** check `1.c` (employer-sponsored EB-2 with approved DOL PERM).

3. **Part 3 — Information About the Person for Whom You Are Filing:**
   - Repeat the applicant's full legal name, U.S. or foreign address, date/country of birth, country of citizenship/nationality, A-Number (if any), and current U.S. arrival/status information (I-94 number, passport number, expiration date, current nonimmigrant status such as F-1, H-1B, O-1, J-1) if inside the United States.

4. **Part 4 — Processing Information:**
   - Select **Adjustment of Status in the U.S.** (`Items 2.a–2.b`) or **Consular Processing abroad** (`Items 1.a–1.c`) consistently with the applicant's physical location and visa strategy.
   - **Item 6** (*Filing without an original labor certification*): Check **Yes** as instructed for EB-1A (not required) or EB-2 NIW (seeking National Interest Waiver with uncertified ETA-9089 components attached).
   - Accurately disclose any prior immigrant petitions (`Item 7`).

5. **Part 5 & Part 6 — Additional Petitioner & Proposed Employment Info:**
   - In Part 5, identify the petitioner as **Self** (Individual Self-Petitioner) and leave employer-only financial fields (`Net Annual Income`, `Current Number of U.S. Employees`) N/A or as instructed for self-petitioners.
   - In Part 6, provide the accurate **Job Title**, **6-digit SOC Code**, and **Nontraditional Description of the Job** consistent with the Personal Statement and supporting evidence.

6. **Part 8 — Petitioner's Statement & Signature:**
   - For paper Lockbox filings, the self-petitioner must sign in **original handwritten wet ink**.

## 2. Mandatory Uncertified FLAG Form ETA-9089 Three-Part Package for EB-2 NIW

Under `8 C.F.R. § 204.5(k)(4)(ii)` and official USCIS guidance for the revised FLAG Form ETA-9089, **EB-2 NIW self-petitioners must include three specific uncertified DOL form components directly with Form I-140** (downloaded as PDFs and submitted to USCIS, *not* filed through DOL's FLAG system):

1. **Form ETA-9089, Application for Permanent Employment Certification** (2-page main form; leave employer/prevailing-wage sections blank or N/A as applicable for a self-petitioner, and complete **Section D — Foreign Worker Information** indicating Appendix A is attached);
2. **Form ETA-9089, Appendix A — Foreign Worker Information** (complete education, training, skills, licenses, and qualifying employment/experience history matching the CV, diplomas, transcripts, and experience letters 100%); and
3. **Form ETA-9089, Final Determination — Permanent Employment Certification Approval** (complete and sign with a **handwritten wet signature** under **Section B — Foreign Worker Declaration**, declaring under penalty of perjury that Appendix A is true and accurate).

*(Note: EB-1A petitions do **not** require Form ETA-9089, Appendix A, or Final Determination.)*

## 3. Current Fee Schedule (2024–2026), Separate Payments & Filing Routes

*(Always re-verify against live [USCIS Form G-1055](https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055) and [Form I-140](https://www.uscis.gov/i-140) before mailing or uploading.)*

| Fee Item | Current Reference Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Form I-140 Base Fee** | **$715** (paper) / **$665** (where direct online intake applies) | Primary petition fee |
| **Asylum Program Fee (Self-Petitioner)** | **$300** | Requires checking **Part 1, Q5 = No, Q6 = Yes** on Form I-140 |
| **Form I-907 Premium Processing (Optional)** | **$2,965** *(effective March 1, 2026)* | **15 business days** for EB-1A (`E11`); **45 business days** for EB-2 NIW (`E21`) |

### Critical Payment & Assembly Rules
1. **Separate Payment Instruments:** Submit a **separate check / money order / Form G-1450 (credit card) / Form G-1650 (ACH)** for each fee component ($715 I-140 fee, $300 Asylum Program Fee, and optional $2,965 I-907 fee). Never combine I-140 and I-907 on a single payment instrument.
2. **Paper Lockbox Assembly Order:**
   `Form G-1145 -> Separate Payment Forms (G-1450 / G-1650 / Checks) -> Form I-907 (if used) -> Form I-140 -> Uncertified ETA-9089 + Appendix A + Signed Final Determination (NIW only) -> Master Table of Contents & Exhibit Index -> I-140 Petition Brief -> Exhibit A (Identity, Degrees, CV, Signed Personal Statement) -> Exhibit B (Objective Evidence with Cover Sheets) -> Exhibit C (Signed Recommendation Letters + CVs)`.
3. **Scanner-Safe Physical Formatting vs. myUSCIS Online Upload Rules:**
   - **Paper Lockbox:** Print single-sided on 8.5" × 11" white paper; keep a **1.0-inch clear top margin** on every page (USCIS two-hole punches packets at the top). Because Lockbox scanners convert pages to high-contrast grayscale (`IOE` electronic file), **do not rely on faint yellow highlighter alone**—use **bold red/dark vector boxes and margin callout labels** around key sentences on exhibits.
   - **myUSCIS Online Upload:** Each uploaded PDF must be **$\le 12\text{ MB}$** (use Ghostscript `-dPDFSETTINGS=/ebook` if needed), unencrypted, with clean alphanumeric filenames, and organized into the portal's evidence categories with source URLs and access dates visible on web-derived exhibits.

## 4. RFE & NOID Surgical Diagnostic & Response Playbook

When a Request for Evidence (RFE) or Notice of Intent to Deny (NOID) arrives, place the full notice in `post-filing/notice/` and execute this four-step protocol:

1. **Verbatim Audit (`post-filing/notice/issue-map.md`):**
   - Record the **Service Center** (TSC vs. NSC), **Officer Code**, **Issue Date**, and **Hard Physical Receipt Deadline** (NOIDs typically have a strict 30-day window).
   - Lock in every criterion or prong **already granted/conceded** by the officer in Section I of the response brief.
2. **Classify Each Challenged Point:**
   - **Type A — Overlooked Existing Evidence (Officer Boilerplate Error):** Politely pinpoint the exact Original Exhibit and page (`[Original Exhibit B-4, p. 2]`) and attach a re-highlighted copy as a Supplemental Exhibit.
   - **Type B — Improper Legal / Policy Standard:** Rebut with binding authorities (*Matter of Chawathe* on preponderance; *Kazarian*, *Buletini*, *Chursov*, *Rubin*, *Zizi*, and *Mukherji v. Miller* (2026) on EB-1A; *Matter of Dhanasar* and `6 USCIS-PM F.5` on NIW).
   - **Type C — Genuine Evidentiary Gap:** Collect targeted new corroboration while strictly respecting the **filing-date eligibility cutoff (`8 C.F.R. § 103.2(b)(1)`, *Matter of Katigbak*, *Matter of Izummi*)**—use post-filing evidence to corroborate pre-existing standing or continuing impact of pre-filing work, never to pass off post-filing achievements as pre-filing threshold qualification.
3. **Candid Response vs. Withdraw-and-Refile Assessment:**
   - If the applicant's decisive breakthroughs (e.g., a major new award, promotion, or citation surge) occurred *after* the original filing date and the visa bulletin / nonimmigrant status permits, evaluate whether responding, refiling, or filing a parallel petition is the strongest strategic move.
4. **Draft the Response Package (`post-filing/response/`):**
   - Place the original RFE/NOID notice on top, followed by `01-rfe-response-letter.md` (mirroring the exact order of the officer's notice) and `02-supplemental-exhibit-index.md` (`Supplemental Exhibit S-1`, `S-2`...).

## 5. Post-Denial Remedy vs. Refile Overhaul Protocol (Zero Minimal-Diff Inertia)

When a case receives a USCIS Denial Notice:
1. **Separate Remedy/Deadline Check from Refile Drafting:**
   - Immediately record the denial issue date and the **30-calendar-day (33 days if mailed) Form I-290B window** (`8 C.F.R. § 103.3` / `§ 103.5`) and evaluate any immediate impact on underlying nonimmigrant status or I-485 pending status. Do not let refile drafting obscure time-sensitive remedy deadlines.
2. **Preserve Historical Baselines Immutably:**
   - Keep the original filed `final/` packet, signed recommendation letters, and any submitted RFE response untouched as historical records. Never overwrite or mutate them in place.
3. **Eliminate Minimal-Diff Inertia (`No T-DIFF Patching`):**
   - Never treat a pre-denial draft as sacred or attempt to fix a denial with cosmetic word edits. Any criterion theory, role description, or Final Merits framing that the adjudicator rejected must be **structurally rebuilt from the ground up** (or replaced with a stronger criterion/evidence chain) before refiling.

## 6. Source-Bound Translations, Identity Variants, Retrogression & Cloud-Sync Privacy

1. **Source-Bound Certified Translations (`8 C.F.R. § 103.2(b)(3)`) vs. Foreign Notarization:**
   - Every non-English exhibit relied upon (award certificates, grant task books, patents, tax records, media articles, employer letters) must be individually bound in `04-exhibit-index.md` to: (a) the complete foreign-language copy, (b) a **complete word-for-word English translation** (never a summary or partial extract), and (c) a **signed Certificate of Translation Accuracy** attesting to translator competency and accuracy.
   - **Trap to avoid:** Generic translation language buried once in the brief does **not** satisfy `8 C.F.R. § 103.2(b)(3)`, and a foreign government notarization (e.g., Chinese 公证书) is **not** a substitute for the signed USCIS translator certification.
2. **Identity & Publication Name-Variant Reconciliation:**
   - Verify consistency across: (a) legal passport name, (b) native-script name, (c) hyphenated/unhyphenated pinyin or maiden/married name variants on publications and patents, (d) Google Scholar / ORCID / Web of Science profile ownership, and (e) employment dates/titles across CV, petition brief, and recommendation letters.
3. **Visa Bulletin Retrogression, AOS vs. Consular, Cross-Chargeability & CSPA:**
   - For applicants born in oversubscribed chargeability areas (e.g., China-mainland or India), **Form I-140 approval locks the Priority Date (`8 C.F.R. § 204.5(e)`) but does not itself authorize I-485 Adjustment of Status filing** unless the priority date is current under USCIS's monthly designated adjustment chart (*Final Action Dates* vs. *Dates for Filing*).
   - Screen upfront for: (a) maintaining valid underlying nonimmigrant status (H-1B, O-1, F-1/OPT) in the U.S., (b) **Spouse Cross-Chargeability (`INA § 202(b)(2)`)** if the applicant's spouse was born in a non-retrogressed country, and (c) **Child Status Protection Act (CSPA)** age-out risks for derivative children approaching age 21.
4. **Cloud-Sync (OneDrive / Dropbox / iCloud) PII Leak Prevention:**
   - Never store active case folders containing raw passports, I-94s, tax returns, W-2s, or unredacted USCIS notices inside cloud-synced desktop folders (`OneDrive`, `Dropbox`, `iCloudDrive`, `Google Drive`) unless explicitly intended; prefer a local non-synced directory (such as `C:\Dev\OpenPetition` or `~/dev/OpenPetition`).
